九、持久化与G1前少数必须Native实证的风险
lead/customer FK restrict、lead_no unique、enum/非负amount/score0..100 CHECK、row_version version()。owner及converted uuid无新FK/unique；owner record lead FK restrict，previous/new owner裸uuid；snapshot/override lead FK cascade，snapshot override/sourceRecord裸uuid，jsonb components/gate。override pending/approved各partial unique lead_id；supersededById无FK。不改原迁移。

W2：create/update/convert实际SQL及query-triggered auto-flush，带附件copy时audit flush是否提前写project/lead/link；强制链接/snapshot/audit失败，逐个分列已写/未写与回滚。
